Before you write to us

A few things that come up often:

  • Changing or cancelling an order — most orders leave us within 24 hours, so we can only make changes while your parcel is still with us. Email as soon as you can with your order number. Custom Couture orders can be changed until production begins.
  • Something wrong with your set — email us within 7 days of delivery with photographs. We'll put it right with a replacement, a partial refund or a full refund, whichever fits. In most cases you won't need to post anything back.
  • Sizing — we're not able to accept returns or exchanges because a size doesn't fit, so please check the Size Chart on the product page before ordering. If you're between sizes or unsure, message us first — we'd much rather spend five minutes helping you choose.
  • Handmade variation — small differences in tone, placement and finish between sets are normal, and are what makes each one handmade rather than machine-stamped. Genuine defects are a different matter and are always covered.
  • Delivery times — within the U.S., orders usually arrive 3–6 business days after dispatch. International times vary by destination and by the service chosen at checkout; you'll see a live estimate before you pay.
